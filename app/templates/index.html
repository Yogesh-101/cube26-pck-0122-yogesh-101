How a pack check runs

  1. 1

    Photograph the open package from the angles that show every item.

  2. 2

    Enter the order lines the box is supposed to contain.

  3. 3

    One batched model call runs all five checks on the unit.

  4. 4

    You get SEAL, STOP & FIX, or NEEDS REVIEW when the evidence will not support a call.

  5. 5

    Override when you disagree. The original verdict and your reason are both kept.
